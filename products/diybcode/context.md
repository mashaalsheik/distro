# DIYbcode: context

> Drafted from the [DIYbcode app README](https://github.com/mashaalsheik/DIYbcode). Everything marked `unknown` or "to confirm" needs your answer before a brief can use it.

## What it is

A marketplace where businesses post the tools they wish existed, and builders pitch to build them. A need can be posted in the app, or by tagging the bot on X (`@diybcode I need a tool that…`, or a bare tag under someone else's post to import that post). Builders browse the feed, upvote needs ("I need this too"), and pitch with a portfolio or demo link.

## Audience

Two sides; each post should pick one.

- **Business owners and operators** with a repetitive problem they'd pay to have solved but can't build themselves. Example from the seed data: a salon owner who wants an AI to answer calls and book appointments.
- **Builders** (indie developers, freelancers, people building with AI coding tools) looking for problems that someone has already said they'd pay for.

To confirm: which side you're growing first.

## Problems we can speak to

- Businesses: "I know what tool I need but don't know who could build it or what it would cost."
- Builders: "I can build things fast now, but I don't know what to build that someone actually wants."
- Builders: validating an idea before building it (upvotes on a need as demand signal).

## Factual constraints

Features that exist in the app today:

- Post a need in the app; title, category and budget are filled in automatically if left blank.
- Tag the bot on X to post a need, or to import the post you're replying to (credited to its original author).
- Import a post on X by pasting its link.
- Upvote, filter and sort needs; builders send pitches with a portfolio or demo link.
- The poster can mark a need *Being built* or *Built ✓*.
